Our Vision

To cultivate a society where educational parity and quality healthcare are fundamental rights, fostering self-reliant rural communities across India.

Our Mission

Empowering marginalized sectors through scholarship programs, specialized medical camps, and holistic rural infrastructure development.

Our Legacy & Journey

Founded on May 4th, 2023, the JNANA PEETA EDUCATIONAL AND CHARITABLE TRUST emerged from a shared commitment to uplift the rural landscapes of Mandya, Karnataka. Recognizing the significant disparities in access to modern educational tools and specialized medical attention, our founders envisioned a structured platform for impactful philanthropy.

Over the past years, we have transitioned from a small volunteer group into a registered trust that partners with government bodies and medical professionals. Our history is etched with the stories of students who received their first computers and elderly villagers who regained their vision through our surgical camps. We continue to operate with radical transparency, ensuring that every rupee donated contributes directly to the betterment of our community through structured rural development modules and financial literacy workshops.

Chairman's Perspective

“Progress in rural development is not measured by the infrastructure we build, but by the confidence we instill in our youth. At Jnana Peeta, we are committed to dismantling the barriers that prevent talent from blooming in our villages. Our focus remains steadfast on sustainability and long-term community impact. We believe that by providing a strong foundation in education and health, we are not just helping individuals but elevating entire families out of generational cycles of poverty.”

Founder's Mission

“Having grown up in the heart of Karnataka, I understood that empathy without action is incomplete. Jnana Peeta was founded to turn that empathy into a movement. Every medical camp we organize and every scholarship we award is a step toward an equitable society where geography doesn't determine destiny. Our mission is to ensure that the rural talent pool gets the same professional exposure and financial guidance as their urban counterparts, creating a balanced and progressive India.”
