Rural Education Programs

We bridge the digital divide by providing computers, high-quality study materials, and scholarship funds to talented students in remote villages of Mandya.

Specialized Health Camps

Access to specialized medical care is often limited in rural areas. Our trust organizes regular eye-checkup camps, cardiac screenings, and general wellness programs with top-tier doctors.
