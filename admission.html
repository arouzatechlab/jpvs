Admissions Guidelines

Every parent shall visit and fill the enquiry form first. Parents are advised not to fill more than one enquiry form for one applicant.

Admission is on First Come First Serve Basis.

Admission to Grades Pre KG and KG through formal interaction.

Admission to Grades I and above based on written test.

Final decision of admission will be of the School based on the Test/Interaction.

Registration charges are non-refundable & need to be paid ONE time only.

Documents Required

Birth Certificate of the child.
Residence Address Proof (Aadhar/Passport).
Aadhar Card (Child & Parents).
School Leaving Certificate.
6 Passport size photographs.
Blood group report.
