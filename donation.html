Common Questions

How can I donate to JNANA PEETA TRUST?

You can donate via direct bank transfer using the details provided above. We also accept cheques and demand drafts in favor of "JNANA PEETA EDUCATIONAL AND CHARITABLE TRUST" payable at Mandya.

Is my donation eligible for tax benefits?

Yes, all donations to our trust are eligible for tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. After your donation, please email us your transaction details and PAN number to receive the tax receipt.

Where will my donation be used?

Your contributions are primarily used for our ongoing educational scholarships, funding rural health camps, and maintaining our support programs for local orphanages and elderly care centers.

Can I support specific programs?

Absolutely. You can mention the specific program (e.g., "Education Fund" or "Health Camp") in the transaction remarks, or contact our trustees to discuss how you would like your contribution to be utilized.
